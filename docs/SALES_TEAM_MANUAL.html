OptCulture Sales Docs – Team Manual

How to create a Proposal, and then the Quotation & Order Form and the Master Services Agreement (MSA), in the OptCulture Sales Docs app. Version 1.0, November 2026.

1. Before you start

  • Open https://salestools.optculturecx.com and sign in with your @optculture.com Google account. Use a browser window where only that account is signed in.
  • If you see "Your account is not set up yet", ask Amith to add you to the Users list.
  • You see your own deals. Managers also see their team's deals. Amith sees everything and approves special pricing and MSA exceptions.
  • All documents are created as PDFs. You receive them by email and can open them from the deal. You never edit the documents directly: to change anything, edit the deal in the app and generate again.

2. How a deal moves

  1. Create the deal and generate the Proposal. The Proposal shows packages and unit prices, without totals.
  2. Send the Proposal to the client and mark the deal Sent (My Deals, the ⋯ menu).
  3. When the client accepts, click Convert to Order Form.
  4. Complete the contract details and generate the Quotation & Order Form and the MSA. The Order Form cites the Proposal number and the MSA number; the MSA cites the Order Form number. The Terms of Service, Data Processing Agreement and Service Level Agreement are not generated: the MSA includes them as links to optculture.com.
  5. If a price is below standard or the MSA has exceptions, the documents are created as drafts and Amith is asked to approve. After approval you receive the final PDFs.
  6. When the client signs, mark the deal Won. Later purchases by the same client are done as a Top-up (section 8).

3. Create a Proposal

Click New Deal. The deal has four steps. You can move between steps at any time and click Save draft whenever you like.

Step 3 · Proposal

  • Language: English (Arabic is available only where a template exists).
  • Title and tagline: optional; a default title is used if left empty.
  • Executive summary and journeys: optional but strongly recommended. Write two or three short paragraphs about the client's goals, and one journey per line.
  • Show consulting rates: leave ticked unless you don't want consulting rates in the Proposal.

Step 4 · Review and generate

  • Check the review. If anything is missing, the What's missing box lists it; click Fix → to jump to the field.
  • If you entered a special price, add a short Note to Amith explaining why.
  • Click Generate proposal. Wait for the confirmation screen: it shows the PDF link and then returns you to My Deals.
  • You receive one email with the PDF attached. If approval is needed, the PDF is a DRAFT: do not send it to the client until Amith approves and you receive the final PDF.

What will be generated. The box in step 3 tells you what you will get:

  • New client: two documents, the Quotation & Order Form and the MSA.
  • Client who already has an MSA (an earlier deal was marked Won): the Quotation & Order Form only, citing the existing MSA. Tick "Issue a new MSA anyway" only if a new MSA is required, for example for a new entity.

MSA special terms and exceptions. Type each agreed exception on its own line and name the clause it changes, for example: "Clause 3 (Term and Termination): cure period of 45 days instead of 30." Leave the box empty if there are none; the MSA then prints "None." Anything typed here goes to Amith for approval, and the exceptions apply to all Order Forms under the MSA.

Step 4 · Review and generate

  • Fix anything listed in What's missing.
  • Click Generate Order Form & MSA (or Generate Order Form for an existing client).
  • You receive one email with the PDFs attached. Review them before sending to the client.
  • When the client signs, mark the deal Won.

6. Approvals

Amith's approval is needed when:

  • a subscription, one-time or messaging price is below the standard price;
  • the MSA has special terms or exceptions;
  • a top-up licence price is below the contracted price, or a top-up includes WhatsApp credits.

What happens:

  • The documents are created as DRAFT PDFs, marked "Draft – pending approval – not for client use". Do not send them.
  • Amith receives an email and sees only the items that need approval, with the standard and proposed prices side by side.
  • When he approves, the final PDFs are created and emailed to you automatically.
  • If he rejects, you receive an email with his comment and the deal shows Rejected. Make the changes and generate again.
  • If you change an approved price or the exceptions text afterwards, approval is needed again.

7. Changes, versions and status

  • To change a generated document, open the deal from My Deals, edit it and generate again. A new version is created, and the previous version is archived.
  • The Generated documents table on each deal lists every version with its date and a PDF link.
  • Keep the status up to date from the ⋯ menu: Sent, Won or Lost. Deals with no update for 60 days are highlighted as needing follow-up.
  • Use Clone as new deal to start a similar deal for another client.

8. Top-ups for existing clients

When a client who has signed (deal marked Won) wants more, open the ⋯ menu on that deal and click Top-up. A new deal opens on "Top-up items".

  • Enter only what the client is buying now: additional stores or e-commerce sites (at the contracted price), new add-ons, communication packs, WhatsApp credits (type, number of credits and price per credit) or optional services. At least one item is mandatory.
  • In step 3, set the top-up's own start date, term and billing cycle.
  • Generate to create a Top-up Quotation & Order Form under the client's existing MSA. WhatsApp credits always need Amith's approval.

9. Questions

  • The Generate button is grey. Open the Review step and check the What's missing box.
  • I can't find a deal. In My Deals, check the date filter (set it to All time) and the status filter.
  • The client wants different wording in the MSA. Add it as a special term or exception in step 3. Amith will review it.
  • The currency is wrong. The currency follows the client's country: INR for India, ZAR for South Africa, USD everywhere else.
  • Anything else: contact Varaprasad.
