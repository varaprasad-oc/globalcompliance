{{ClientShortName}} Unified Loyalty Program

{{TEXT:MultiBrandIntro}}

One Member. One Wallet. Every Brand, Every Store & Online.

Every customer who enrols anywhere in the {{ClientShortName}} ecosystem — at any store or online — becomes a member of a single program. Their profile, points balance, tier status and preferences are shared across every brand and touchpoint from the moment of enrolment.

How Earn & Burn Works Across Brands & Channels

  • Earn anywhere: Members earn points on every qualifying purchase at any store, across every brand, and on every online order.
  • Redeem everywhere: Points are a single group-wide currency, redeemable in-store or online at any brand, with redemption rates configurable per brand, channel or category.
  • Differential earn rates: {{ClientShortName}} retains full control to set different earn rates by brand, channel or category to drive repeat purchase and protect margin.
  • Brand- & campaign-funded mechanics: Individual brands or campaigns can fund their own bonus-point drives or category accelerators, with full visibility of the cost and uplift attributable to each.
  • Tier benefits on every brand & channel: Tier status is recognised across every brand and channel, driving cross-brand shopping and share-of-wallet.

Cross-Brand Settlement & Liability Management

The platform tracks point liability at issuing-store and brand level and redemption at redeeming-store, brand or channel level. OptCulture provides month-end settlement reports so Finance can reconcile point movements across every brand, store and online store transparently.
