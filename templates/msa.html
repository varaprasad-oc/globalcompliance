3. Term and Termination

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and remains in effect until terminated in accordance with its provisions.

Each Order Form shall govern its own subscription duration, renewal, cancellation and payment obligations.

Either Party may terminate this Agreement or an affected Order Form if the other Party materially breaches its obligations and fails to remedy the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice. Immediate termination is permitted where required by law or where the breach is incapable of remedy.

Termination of this Agreement shall terminate all active Order Forms unless otherwise agreed. Termination of an individual Order Form shall not automatically terminate this Agreement or other active Order Forms.

Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice when no Order Form remains active.

Accrued obligations shall survive termination, and the handling of Customer Data shall be governed by the DPA.

4. Order of Precedence

In the event of any inconsistency between the contractual documents:

The SLA shall govern service availability, support and applicable remedies.

The ToS shall govern all remaining matters concerning the Services.
